# Fonts

Hind Siliguri (Regular, SemiBold, Bold) by the Indian Type Foundry, licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). It covers Bangla and Latin, so one family is used for both
languages, in Flutter widgets and in Flame text alike.

Galada (Regular) by Pablo Impallari and Black Foundry, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, from
Google Fonts. A hand-lettered Bangla display face, used only for big titles on the menus (the game's name,
characters' names, page titles, the end of a day).
